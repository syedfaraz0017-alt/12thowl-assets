1. Introduction

Welcome to OwlTrack, a student attendance management application developed by HYDRO SF. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, OwlTrack may collect:

  • Name and email address.

User information may be stored on secure cloud services or on the user's device, depending on the features used. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access and disclosure.

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized educational administrators, necessary service providers, or when legally required.

OwlTrack may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, analytics, crash reporting and other application functionality. Such services process information according to their respective privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

OwlTrack may be used by students under 18. Where required by applicable law, appropriate parental or guardian consent must be obtained before collecting children's personal information.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as necessary for application services and applicable legal requirements. Users can request account and data deletion by contacting our support email.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information.
